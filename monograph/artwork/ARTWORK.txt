THE THIELE MACHINE — COVER AND PART ILLUSTRATIONS

These are new AI-generated illustrations in the engraving tradition of
Gustave Dore. They are not historical prints by Dore. They were generated
with the built-in image_gen tool and are included locally so the book can
be built without a network connection or an image-generation service.

Cover: cover-window.png
A small observer faces a narrow opening in a large dark wall. The framing
offers a view while leaving most of the surrounding space unseen. It is a
literary image for a restricted observation, not a claim about a physical
higher dimension. The title and author are set in LaTeX, not in the bitmap.

Part I: part-1-retention.png — The cut remains.
A close, low view of a split tally shows the matching cuts retained in the
wood. The book introduces tally marks in A record and a toll and returns to
the historical split tally in The counter. This plate emphasizes retention;
it asserts no numerical price, historical reconstruction or heat measurement.

Part II: part-2-perspective.png — The sphere, seen from outside the plane.
The external reader sees a sphere intersecting a plane. This is the reader's
oblique view of the construction, not an inhabitant's in-plane line of sight.
Windows and shadows explains the different access supplied by seeing, feeling
and changing slices. The illustration supplies neither the time sequence nor
an inference from one section to a uniquely determined solid.

Part III: part-3-established.png — Checking comes before the record.
An over-the-shoulder view shows an artisan checking a corner with a square,
with a blank ledger waiting beside the work. It illustrates examination
before recording. A completed check is not inferred from an idle notebook.
The scene is an analogy for the distinction developed in What established
means, not a depiction of the native CHECK/COMMIT/CERTIFY implementation or
a claim that one measurement certifies every property of the object.

Part IV: part-4-scope.png — Each opening leaves something out.
An oblique interior view shows several openings onto one landscape. The
stone between them hides parts of that landscape. The relation to The claims
and their conditions and Scope is that each view has a limited domain.
Nothing here depicts the open physical or priority claims as proved.

The large plates appear only at part openings. Chapter and appendix headings
use typography and a short rule, preserving clear space for the text. The
existing exact reasoning diagrams remain separate and unchanged.

The earlier cube/card generated study is not included: its cast-shadow
geometry was not sufficiently controlled for that demonstration. The earlier
rejected plane/window decoration is also no longer used in this edition.

Generation prompts: generation-prompts.json and cover-detail-prompt.txt.
Manifest: manifest.json, including native pixel dimensions and SHA-256.
Cover is 1024 by 1536 pixels; the requested detail pass did not deliver the
requested 2400 by 3600 pixels. At the current full-page width its native
resolution is about 138 pixels per inch. The four part plates are 1448 by
1086 pixels, about 325 pixels per inch at the book's text width. No artificial
resampling is represented as recovered detail. A press-specific cover master
would need the printer's resolution and bleed specification; the present PDF
is the book edition, not a separately specified commercial print wrap.
