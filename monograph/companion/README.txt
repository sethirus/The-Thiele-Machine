Companion lookup material — not included in the book build.

The book contains its mathematical definitions, constructions and arguments.
These files locate and reproduce optional software checks.

source-statements.tex: literal proof-assistant declarations and comparisons.
source-index.tex: source locations and supplementary citation concordance.
citation-concordance.txt: original source-reference contexts.
tool-history-sources.tex: bibliography for the checking tools.

The archived TeX extracts retain their original section references and are not
standalone documents. Checking procedures and the release receipt are in
../COMPANION_CHECKS.txt.
