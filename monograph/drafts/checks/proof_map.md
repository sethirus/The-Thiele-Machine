# Proof map

Every line of the ledger in chapter twenty-two, with its kind, the Python check that tests it, and the Gallina
theorems that prove it. "closed" means Print Assumptions reports "Closed under the global context". "Coq
real-number axioms" means the theorem uses only the two axioms the Coq standard library builds its real numbers
on (ClassicalDedekindReals.sig_forall_dec and functional extensionality). Lines whose kind is chosen, assumed,
cited, thesis or not claimed need no proof; they are listed so the map covers the whole ledger. The book cites
none of this; the arguments are on its pages.
