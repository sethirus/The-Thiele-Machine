THE THIELE MACHINE — TYPOGRAPHY WORKING SETTING
Devon Thiele

Devon's wording and organization are fixed. The task is the visual art of
composing that existing work on the page.

The entire original document body is passed to LaTeX verbatim, including
the title page, contents, paragraphs, headings, references, order, and
explicit breaks. No passage-specific formatting is inserted into it.

The earlier paired openings, recurring entry positions, added pauses,
shortened contents, and revised front matter have been removed. That
experiment is archived under archive/prior-structural-draft and is
superseded. Its reading-copy PDF and verification report describe that
earlier experiment only.

Current work is limited to the preamble: typography, measure, leading,
spacing, margins, running matter, and visual hierarchy. Natural line and
page wrapping follows those choices. This working setting does not claim
the artistic design is finished.

Rebuild with:
    python build.py
    python review.py

Verification checks exact document-body equality and preservation of the
existing numbered labels. It requires PyMuPDF and ../monograph.aux. The
build requires pdfLaTeX and the original manuscript's packages plus those
in book.sty. Construction checks do not certify artistic success.
