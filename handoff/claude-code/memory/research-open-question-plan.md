Devon (2026-09-27) asked whether the whole project is arbitrary without an answer to the open question (does computation/nature in general carry the μ receipt, i.e. is A2 more than a chosen premise?). He wants to pursue it "until we can prove the answer definitively", prepared now, worked later, never breaking the repo. Keep research findings in chat/memory or a private page, not repo files.

Honest framing agreed: the math results (verifier/decoder, pricing) can be proved outright; "μ is physical cost" is empirical and needs a physical premise; the big claim can only be won by stacking load-bearing results.

Literature found (do not reprove these):
- Shadow/decoder theorem ("query constant on each observation class") is essentially Halpern–Moses knowledge: a process knows φ iff φ holds in every run consistent with its view (JACM 1990, https://arxiv.org/pdf/cs/0006009). Novelty must come from adding cost.
- Existing repo reductions (coq/kernel/reductions/: GasMetering, PoSFinality, ProofCarryingVerifier, TEEAttestation, TransparencyLog) restate known folk theorems in kernel vocabulary (their own headers say so). Consistency checks, not new results.
- Coiteux-Roy & Wolf 2022, "Unconditional Proofs-of-Work and Other Possibilities of Thermodynamic Cryptography" (https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.13278): players have unbounded computation, reversible ops free, only erasure (reset to blank) costs free energy via Landauer; proof of thermodynamic work = handing over a blank register; Thm 1: cannot create blank qubits without free energy except w.p. 2^-s. Closest neighbour to "μ as physical cost".
- PoSW/VDF lower bounds in the random-oracle model (https://eprint.iacr.org/2019/663.pdf): limits on what a verifier can infer about sequential work.
- Bennett logical depth (history of computation) and Bennett/Landauer: measurement/knowledge gain can be free; erasure is what costs.

Key insight to build on: loose A2 ("coming to know costs") conflicts with Bennett (knowing can be free). Sharp A2 ("certification = committing: collapsing indistinguishable alternatives into one irreversible record") is many-to-one, which is exactly what Landauer charges.

Candidate load-bearing theorem (Coq-provable core is a counting fact): in a system where reversible steps are bijections and cost 0 and only irreversible (erasure) steps cost, any certification that collapses N indistinguishable alternatives to one committed state must be irreversible and cost ≥ log2 N erased bits. Would turn A2 from a chosen premise into a consequence of Landauer + a precise definition of certification as commitment. Combine with Halpern–Moses knowledge semantics for "indistinguishable alternatives".

Next steps when resumed: (1) check whether this exact theorem is already in thermodynamics-of-computation literature (Bennett 1982/2003, Landauer 1961, Wolpert "stochastic thermodynamics of computation", Coiteux-Roy–Wolf); (2) define certification-as-commitment formally against the existing CertificationSystem record; (3) prove the counting core in Coq (finite state, bijective free steps, erasure count); (4) then the verifier (direction 1) and blockchain (direction 2) angles, each needing one result the field lacks.
