## Ground-truth result

The universal claim that every latchable reading preserves all five bundled
main results is false. The graph-allocation reading
`1 <=? pg_next_id (vm_graph s)` is permanent because graph allocation never
decreases the next module identifier. Starting from `init_state`, the
zero-cost instruction `PNEW [] 0` switches that reading from false to true.
The writer therefore contradicts the `priced` conjunct.

This counterexample satisfies the intended register-width invariant:
`item1_3_init_register_width` proves that the register list of `init_state` has
length `REG_COUNT`, which is 16. It does not use the empty-register witness
from the broader Item 1.2 audit.

For `certification_reading`, latchability and all five bundled main results
hold. The proof separately establishes permanence, a concrete `CERTIFY 0`
write, the positive instruction-cost floor, non-recoverability through both
projections, and inexactness of both shadow-price accounts.
