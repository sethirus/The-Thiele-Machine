# Part 3, Item 3.1, Round 3 freeze: internal guest recursion

Date: 2026-10-01

## Definitions held fixed

- The language is the existing twelve-constructor `GInstr` fragment in
  `Kernel.VMSelfGuest`; register fields are restricted to 0 through 3 and
  register values are arbitrary natural numbers.
- Execution is the existing `g_run`/`g_beh` semantics, proved to be actual
  `run_vm_u` execution. No opcode, register, state field, or semantic rule is
  added.
- Program codes are `guest_program_code p := program_to_nat (g_program p)`.
- Program equivalence is the existing `g_equiv`, including all four final
  guest registers and the guest mu ledger.
- Transformer representation and the goal are exactly
  `g_represents_transformer` and `vm_guest_recursion_theorem` from
  `Kernel.VMRecursionTarget`.

## Exact statement

```coq
Theorem vm_guest_recursion_theorem_closed : vm_guest_recursion_theorem.
```

The theorem must be closed under the global context and must be derived from
guest execution. A host-level Coq decoder, evaluator, specialization
constructor, or choice of the desired fixed point does not discharge it.

## Architectural correction

The proposed four-register evaluator is admissible only with an injective
encoding of arbitrary natural tuples. Fixed 64-bit lanes are not such an
encoding: the guest registers are unbounded naturals. A proof may instead use
an unbounded pairing/sequence representation, a verified two-counter
universal machine compiled by `cm2_compile`, or a direct four-register
interpreter whose representation theorem covers every well-formed guest
program.

## Predicted outcome

BLOCKED is more likely than PROVED in this round. The repository contains a
guest compiler for each fixed two-counter program, but no verified universal
two-counter evaluator or compiler from the numeric `GInstr` encoding to a
self-contained four-register evaluator. The fixed host interpreter cannot be
reused because it occupies registers 4 through 15. This prediction does not
weaken the target.

## Success

Success requires all of the following:

1. concrete well-formed guest programs implementing the internal operations
   used by diagonalization;
2. execution theorems for the internal evaluator and numeric specializer;
3. a closed proof of `vm_guest_recursion_theorem_closed`;
4. `Print Assumptions vm_guest_recursion_theorem_closed` reports `Closed under
   the global context`;
5. the existing proof, vacuity, meaning, prose, and full-build gates pass.

## Failure

The round fails to prove the target if any internal operation is supplied as a
Coq function without a guest program and execution theorem, if fixed-width
packing excludes legal guest states, if the machine or equivalence is changed,
or if the final theorem retains any premise or axiom. After three genuinely
different failed strategies, the unchanged statement is recorded BLOCKED with
the exact missing construction.
