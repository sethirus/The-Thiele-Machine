# The Thiele Machine v3.4.0

The record-axis theorem, its exact limits, the structural and narrowing results, scoped real-system models, cost-framework comparisons, and the physical and prior-work boundaries form one account.

## What carries the weight

- **Every machine can carry the record axis.** Take any deterministic machine and any event it reaches. The machine plus a latch on that event, charging one unit when the latch sets, is an honest extension of it (`latch_core_honest`). A reversible machine with unbounded memory can carry one and stay reversible (`history_latch_honest`). With finite memory it can't (`finite_reversible_cannot_write`).
- **The record can't be read back off the shadow.** On the VM, no function of the named windows returns certification or μ (`cert_not_function_of_forget`, `mu_not_function_of_bare_observable`).
- **On finite hardware, writing a permanent record merges states** (`permanent_flip_is_not_injective`), so an account that prices merges already prices the write (`a2_from_merging_price_and_permanence`).

A record that never switches off and whose update is driven by the base computation is a latch on one event (`uniqueness_round4_holds`). Both premises are essential: revocation and a hidden clock are counterexamples.

## The structural result

- Uniqueness in the form the words suggest is false. A Thiele core that bills CPU time is adequate and runs the VM underneath, and it is not the same machine (`uniqueness_round1_refuted`, `uniqueness_round2_refuted`).
- Up to the price schedule it holds for machines that run the VM and record certification (`uniqueness_round3b_holds`). A state-dependent bill is just a schedule (`surcharged_core_equiv_mod_schedule`).
- Which permanent reading counts as the record is a separate free choice (`uniqueness_round3a_refuted`).
- Over any base, a record that never switches off and whose update is driven by the base computation is a latch on one event (`uniqueness_round4_holds`), and two such records are one axis (`uniqueness_round4_pair_holds`). A revocable record is not a latch (`toggle_not_latch`). A record run by a hidden clock is not driven by the computation (`clock_record_not_driven`).

## Narrowing

- A machine's own spread is priced along a run (`run_narrowing_priced_log`).
- An observer's knowledge can narrow for free (`observer_narrowing_can_be_free`), even read step by step (`demon_refutes_incremental`).
- Three states are enough for free step-by-step narrowing (`free_incremental_narrowing_with_three`), and two are not (`no_free_incremental_narrowing_below_three`).
- No Free Insight keeps its name. The insight it prices is certified insight.

## Cost frameworks

- A graded run carries its total cost as its grade (`run_graded_is_run`).
- The ledger is a writer (`run_writer_is_run_and_cost`).

## Recursion

L, the weak call-by-value lambda calculus, has a second recursion theorem proved from its reduction rules (`second_recursion`, `L_recursion_theorem`), with Rice's theorem (`L_rice`) and the halting problem (`L_halting_undecidable`) following. The twelve-opcode guest VM has numeric decoding, verified fuel-bounded dispatch, and semantic s-m-n specialization (`g_decode_guest_code_roundtrip`, `g_eval_is_actual_vm_execution`, `g_smn`). Its evaluator and numeric specializer are host-level Coq functions, not guest-callable programs; these statements do not establish the VM's internal recursion theorem.

## Exact boundary

- **The pointer question.** Which event a record latches, and why certification is the one to record. Consensus, exact observation, and coordinator-free evolution do not force permanence (`toggle_game_refutes_strong_pointer_necessity`). Durability implies permanence only when persistence is supplied as a premise (`durable_consensus_implies_permanence`).
- **Whether permanence is always paid for at some level:** economic, cryptographic, or physical.
- **Which events every account has to price, beyond merges on finite hardware.**
- **A physical calibration of the ledger.** The two-state calorimeter protocol proves the master equation and exact heat `Delta / 2`. Selecting `Delta = 2 k_B T ln 2` yields the Landauer value; identical population dynamics at another gap yield another heat. Thermal admissibility and device correspondence are premises of any conversion from μ to joules.
- **The VM's own recursion theorem.** Numeric decoding, verified dispatch, and semantic s-m-n are proved. The evaluator and numeric specializer are not guest-callable programs. The recursion theorem proved from operational semantics is for L.
- **A record with many values that only grows.** Threshold-latch decomposition is proved; the one-latch version is refuted.
- **A record revocable at a price, the way Casper's is.** Actual revocation is outside permanence, and price-transfer is refuted.
- **Machines on different bases, compared by cost-respecting simulations.** That is complexity theory's question.
- **Machines that choose at random.** Direct uniqueness up to schedule is refuted. No stronger probabilistic-equivalence theorem is claimed.
- **Certificate Transparency in Coq.** The RFC 9162 iterative inclusion and consistency control flow is modeled and example paths execute. The model excludes collision resistance, signed-tree-head authenticity, and generic completeness.
- **Concrete discrimination machines.** An addressed list-memory RAM core and bounded/unbounded reversible-update fragments are formalized. No complete record-axis adapters or full Janus interpreter are claimed.

## Real-system and observer-map scope

Finite growing records decompose into threshold latches, but not in general
into one latch. Revocable and probabilistic records do not inherit the same
uniqueness theorem. Physical calibration requires thermal and device premises, and the local
graded, writer, potential, and counting correspondences are not full
cost-framework embeddings.

The RFC 9162 verifier covers iterative inclusion and consistency control flow
and examples, not collision resistance or signed-tree-head authenticity. The
PCC, addressed-RAM, reversible-update, and TPM-interface files are scoped
models or countermodels, not complete deployed systems. The twelve-event
observer survey proves its formal classifications and swap behavior. Its
MAC-labelled non-proliferation counterexample depends on the observer model as
a claim about real systems. The two-observer toggle game independently refutes
the proposed implication from consensus and coordinator-free observation to
permanence.

Five narrow external consequences reduce to known local indistinguishability
or durability arguments. Five stronger candidates require protocol or hardware
semantics not supplied here. No surveyed consequence is simultaneously
record-axis dependent, novel, and ready for external use. The pointer criterion
is a conjecture.
