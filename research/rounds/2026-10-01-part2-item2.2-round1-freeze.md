The classification to test is precise. A record that actually changes from
true to false is outside the literal permanent-record axis. It can be inside a
broader accountable-conflict axis when contradiction carries an explicit
penalty. In the Casper model, that penalty is the existence of a quorum whose
votes satisfy a slashing condition. It is not a numeric execution charge, an
executed slash, or a transition-level deletion of a finalized block.
