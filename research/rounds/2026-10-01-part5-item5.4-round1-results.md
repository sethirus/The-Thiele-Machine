# Part 5, Item 5.4 results: TPM quote authenticity

Date: 2026-10-01

Outcome: PARTIAL.

`tpm_interface_authenticity_refuted` is a closed Coq proof that a bare
sign/verify interface does not imply quote authenticity. The concrete scheme
has one key, always signs `false`, and accepts every Boolean signature, so it
accepts `true` although no signing key produces it.

The existing PCR/nonce model therefore cannot remove its authenticity premise
merely by adding signature-shaped fields. Closing the frozen model requires an
additional authenticity or unforgeability premise, or a separately justified
implementation theorem appropriate to the modeled property. That conclusion
is not derivable from the frozen unconstrained sign/verify interface. The PCR
projection impossibility remains assumption-free; authentic origin of the
projection does not.

## Hollowness check

- Definitional: the universal claim is refuted by a concrete interface, not by
  restating its negation.
- Built in: the degenerate verifier is intentionally adversarial and is a valid
  inhabitant of the frozen interface.
- Vacuity: `degenerate_accepts_forgery` supplies the accepted forged value.
- Swap: any secure scheme may satisfy authenticity under stronger premises;
  the theorem concerns what the unconstrained interface alone entails.
- Adversarial read: PASS if the paper says “interface insufficiency,” not “TPM
  signatures are forgeable.”

Literature: the need for cryptographic unforgeability and protected attestation
keys is standard. Wrong prediction: none.
