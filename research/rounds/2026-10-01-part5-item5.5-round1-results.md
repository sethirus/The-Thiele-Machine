# Part 5, Item 5.5 results: full cost-framework embeddings

Date: 2026-10-01

Outcome: BLOCKED.

No full embedding of any of the four named calculi into the actual VM is
present. `CostSemanticsComparison.v` and `CostFrameworks.v` prove useful
fragments: a writer identity, a graded writer trace, the certification
potential lower bound, its exact intersection with a one-bit AARA upper bound,
and a linear-style event-count inequality. No full embedding follows.

Three genuine strategies were attempted and rejected:

1. Reuse the writer/graded trace as the embedding. This lacks source-language
   syntax, typing/effect derivations, semantic preservation, and image
   characterization, so it only embeds a trace-cost fragment.
2. Translate all four through the certification potential. Danner--Licata and
   AARA establish upper bounds while the record law supplies a lower bound;
   the existing equivalence holds only under exact pricing and permanence and
   cannot represent the full analyses.
3. Use certification-event counts as linear resources. `flips_le_cost` proves
   one inequality but has no proof terms, tensor/exponential rules, or
   translation to actual VM programs, so it is not a linear-logic embedding.

What is needed: formal source syntax, judgments, and semantics for one fixed
version of each named framework; actual VM translations; preservation and
reflection; cost correspondence; and image theorems. Until those exist, the
requested equivalent/one-way/incomparable classifications are not proved.
