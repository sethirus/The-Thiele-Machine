Date: 2026-10-01

## Reason for the new round

The first Part 8 draft appended an audit-status paragraph headed
`Ground-truth plan outcome` to each public document. That wording is accurate
as an execution report, but it leaves the public work reading like a sequence
of project-management updates. The user requires the finished repository to
read as a coherent work rather than as a visible work in progress. The Round 1
files remain unchanged as historical evidence.

## Exact statement to establish

The six public documents state the final mathematical boundary directly and
cohesively. They do not describe that boundary as the outcome of a plan, a
numbered Part, a dated audit, or a frozen round. They distinguish proved scope
from unresolved work in ordinary publication language:

1. the central result is the record axis, with certification as one event;
2. threshold decomposition is proved, while single-latch, revocable, and
   probabilistic extensions do not inherit the same uniqueness result;
3. the VM guest Rice reduction is proved but an internal VM recursion theorem
   is not;
4. the physical conversion remains conditional and gives no intrinsic joule
   value to mu;
5. the RFC 9162, PCC, RAM, reversible-update, and TPM results retain their
   exact scoped descriptions;
6. no full embedding of the four cost frameworks is claimed;
7. the twelve-event survey proves only its formal observer-map statements,
   and the real-system pointer criterion remains conjectural; and
8. the external consequence search produced five known narrow countermodels
   and no result satisfying all novelty and applicability conditions.

The dated execution report continues to use the plan's formal outcome
vocabulary and remains the provenance record. It is not a public narrative.
