## Hollowness checks

### Definitional

The 52 `evidence_*` declarations are exact applications or specializations of
existing results. They establish the classification and applicability facts
required by Item 1.1, but they are not new mathematical theorems. The closed
door No Free Insight instance is definitionally transparent and is not counted
as substantive novelty.

### Built in

The primary local pricing model intentionally charges the door-opening event.
Consequently, its exact-pricing applications verify event parametricity rather
than derive the price independently. Separate models discharge the structural
claims: finite permanence and merging, honest erasure accounting, a free
undercharged event, an overcharged event, blind and revealing shadows, record
and history latches, a toggle, and the saturating meter.

`meter_two_ticks_reach_event` close by computation. `door_one_step_costs_one`
and `meter_event_run_pays` establish nonempty paid runs. The guarded full
vacuity sweep checked 852 targets: 852 OK, zero vacuous-true, zero
vacuous-hypothesis, and zero errors. The final four-worker guarded run completed
in 390.7 seconds with 1,453 MB peak Coq RSS. The artifact has SHA-256
`b6a095d9f94bddd777a93586db1a316c4d3b925a0d2e93a576aaca27f56f7909`.

### Swap test

The distinct three-state meter is the structural swap. Its event, transition
shape, and two-step activation differ from the Boolean door, yet the universal
run-cost theorem applies. The toggle separately confirms the revocation branch
when a flip is injective. These checks support event-parametric structure; they
do not generalize any identity classified C.

## Calls made

- The audit unit is a distinct proof identity, while source occurrences remain
  preserved and counted separately.
- Classification is determined from the exact statement, not a theorem name,
  source filename, or document paraphrase.
- G means event-generic statement shape. It does not mean that explicit
  physical, probabilistic, compression, pricing, or injectivity premises have
  been discharged.
- Conditional G applications are labeled PARTIAL even when the application
  itself is a closed Coq term with its conditions universally quantified.
- The current VM schedule is not altered to satisfy the Mu Chaitin functor.
  The concrete counterexample is retained and the functor results remain
  conditional.
- Item 1.1 does not perform Item 1.2's generalization work and does not claim
  the Item 1.3 swap theorem.
- Exact compiled-library hashes are accepted as the reuse boundary for the
  assumption receipt. A changed source or dependency changes the `.vo` hash
  and executes that group again; unchanged groups are not re-proved merely to
  reproduce the same receipt.
- Frozen source and occurrence files remain historical inputs. Mandatory
  current receipt numbers are updated in current publications rather than
  falsifying those numbers to preserve old document hashes.
