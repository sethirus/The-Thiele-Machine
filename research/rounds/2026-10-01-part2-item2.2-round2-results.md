# Part 2, Item 2.2, Round 2 results: constructive Casper accountable safety

Date: 2026-10-01

Outcome: PROVED.

The statement of `accountable_safety` is unchanged. `CasperFFG.v` no longer
imports `Classical` or invokes `classic`, and no equality decision or logical
premise was added to `CasperSetting`. Both
`Print Assumptions accountable_safety` and
`Print Assumptions conflicting_records_are_priced` report `Closed under the
global context`.

The proof now uses `distinct_justified_same_epoch_slashes`: two distinct
justified blocks at the same epoch directly yield a quorum whose members cast
two votes at that epoch. This constructive implication replaces both former
uses of excluded middle. One use had split on equality of abstract hashes; the
other had split on whether a slashed quorum already existed inside the strong
induction.

This changes the Round 1 `casper_conflict_is_accountable` outcome from PARTIAL
to PROVED. It does not change the separate REFUTED result:
`revocation_price_does_not_price_writes` still gives a closed asymmetric-cost
countermodel, so pricing a revocation does not entail pricing the write.

## Hollowness checks

- Definitional: accountable safety is not assumed by the setting. It follows
  from quorum intersection, one-parent hashes, the vote relation, and the
  justification/finalization definitions.
- Built in: fork conditions are premises; the slashed quorum is constructed,
  not supplied. No decidable equality or excluded-middle field was added.
- Vacuity: `chain_setting` remains a closed inhabitant of `CasperSetting`, and
  the one-validator finalization witness still compiles.
- Swap: the theorem remains polymorphic in every `CasperSetting`; the proof
  does not depend on a distinguished validator or concrete hash.
- Adversarial read: the result is accountable safety in the frozen model. It
  proves slashable votes exist, not that a slash was executed, that finality
  was physically deleted, or that writing a finalization record has a cost.
