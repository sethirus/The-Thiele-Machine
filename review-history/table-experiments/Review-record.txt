The Thiele Machine — explanation pass
Author: Devon Thiele

This pass reworks nineteen passages in the newly added appendix material around
operations a reader can perform or trace. It leaves the main chapters, headings,
chapter order, numbered statements and existing displayed equations intact.

The explanations cover copying beans, representing a paper tape with two stacks,
prime packing and its zero case, a predictor confronted with its two answers,
conditional instructions kept folded until selected, finite-budget searching,
pencil projections and conflicting angle demands, signed-card operations,
a deck of local answer plans, and the guest/host example where restoring a value
does not restore an old check's validity.

The tabletop activities explain mechanisms and expose failure cases. Their finite
examples are accompanied by the arguments that extend them. The quantum passage
separates checking the signed-card algebra from realizing the stated shared state
and measurements in a laboratory; it makes no claim to report a performed experiment.
