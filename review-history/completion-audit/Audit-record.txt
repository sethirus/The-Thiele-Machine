Principal mathematical and explanatory corrections
- Finiteness, permanence and a flip force a collision somewhere in the whole
  transition map. The newly entering state need not be one of the colliding
  inputs. Looking only at a flag is not enough to establish a whole-state merge.
- A prepared input distribution determines logical entropy loss. A known start
  yields no positive entropy-loss lower bound; it does not promise zero device
  heat. The text distinguishes a possible collision from its actual use.
- Merge pricing is weaker than halving/worst-case entropy pricing. A one-mark
  charge for a collision does not price an arbitrarily large compression.
- Ledger cost, mean heat, work and entropy production are kept distinct, with
  the physical conditions attached to physical conclusions.
- The explanatory summaries retain clean-start, observation-window, fixed
  claim-language, computability, direct-interface and macro-interface scope.
  The universal program's slowness is not used to infer a limitation of the
  host model or extra computing power in another model.
- The argument remains in the book. Proof-tool output is treated as a check
  of a formal statement, not as the foundation of the conceptual argument or
  as evidence that its physical premises hold.
