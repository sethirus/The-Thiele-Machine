THE THIELE MACHINE — SOURCE REVIEW
12 October 2026

Coverage and evidence
The bibliography inventory contains 662 entries. This review checked citation
identity and metadata across the inventory, followed links, examined flagged
discrepancies, and checked selected attributed quotations against available
texts. A matching title or successful HTTP request is not treated as proof
that a source supports every sentence for which it is cited.

The 242 entries contain 245 DOI references. All 245 were checked against
metadata: 244 through Crossref, and the Forster–Kunze ITP 2019 paper through
DataCite. The three additional DOI references are later journal versions; their
authors, titles, dates and publication data agree with those descriptions. Seventeen initial
flags were examined. Most reflect reprints, incomplete metadata, title markup,
or conference/publication dates. No correction was made solely because an
automated title or date comparison disagreed.

307 distinct non-DOI links were attempted: 294 returned HTTP 200, including two
challenge pages rather than usable texts. Thirteen returned errors. The one
confirmed 404, Nicely's old faculty page, was removed while retaining the journal
citation. The others were access/server errors, not evidence that the cited works
do not exist. The full results, including access failures, are in link-results.json.
The 160 entries without a URL were reviewed and queried bibliographically.
Search results that were reviews, reprints or merely similar titles were not
counted as authentication of the original work.

Corrections
- The cited Justice Department release is dated 2 October 2002 and concerns a
  criminal complaint against Andrew Fastow. The book had called it a 31 October
  indictment release.
- Kent Weare's InfoQ article is cited under its actual displayed title.
- An attributed Hales phrase about frustration and bypassing referees could not
  be substantiated in the cited primary text or targeted searches. The quotation
  was removed. The documented Flyspeck project and its dates remain.
- Eight accessible primary-text links were added to existing bibliography
  entries: Martin-Löf, Corry, Hales, JCGM, Planck, Wheeler, Landauer and Shannon.

Quotation review
Thirty externally attributed passages were selected from the long-quotation
inventory. Twenty-three match their full quoted phrases in the downloaded
corpus after normalization of typography and explicit ellipses. The remaining
seven were resolved as follows:
- Kepler: corroborated in the Cambridge publisher's excerpt of the cited
  historical discussion. The book's disclosure of the secondary route remains;
  this review does not claim to have inspected the original 1611 printing or
  the whole 1966 translation.
- Hales's frustration quotation: removed as unsubstantiated.
- Kempe: wording checked in the transcription of the original 1879 paper.
- Euclid's first common notion: checked in Heath's wording, reproduced by
  Trinity College Dublin. Joyce's online translation uses different wording;
  the book's quotation was not silently changed to that translation.
- Landauer's minimum-achievability sentence: checked on page 187 of the paper
  scan. OCR read 'is' as '1s'; this was not a textual discrepancy.
- Planck: checked in the Clarke–Silberstein 1922 translation, page 11, available
  as Project Gutenberg 33663.
- Berry: checked in Principia Mathematica, volume I, pages 63–64, available as
  Project Gutenberg 78050.

Other downloaded primary texts include Martin-Löf's Siena lectures, JCGM's VIM,
Six's 1782 thermometer paper, Shannon's 1948 paper, Hales's 2008 article, Corry's
chapter, Bennett's 2003 notes, Rovelli's paper, and Knott's reproduction of
Maxwell's note. The quoted 'Call him no more a demon but a valve' sentence is
present in Knott, page 215. The referee-letter passage is present in Hales.

Metadata decisions
- Hellman and Resnik: retain original publication years; the DOI metadata
  describe later paperback/online editions.
- Katsuno–Mendelzon, Kolmogorov and Nipkow: the entries already distinguish the
  original work from the later version associated with the DOI.
- Goguen: retain 1973, matching the journal issue/copyright and standard
  citation, despite the publisher's conflicting 1972 online metadata.
- Wheeler: retain pages 28–40, explicitly confirmed on the author's site;
  Crossref's 33–48 is not used to overwrite the author's bibliography.
- Bell and Bennett's Logical Depth chapter: retain the cited edition's page
  range rather than silently substituting a conflicting metadata/reprint range.
- Lyapunov: the citation describes the full translated work, not one metadata
  record for a component; Wells's conference publication is not its preprint.

Limits of this evidence
This is a bibliography and selected-quotation audit, combined with the separate
manuscript argument audit. It is not a claim to have read all 662 cited works
cover to cover, authenticated every biographical anecdote, or independently
established every priority claim. Access failures and secondary attribution are
recorded explicitly; no successful build or citation lookup removes those limits.
