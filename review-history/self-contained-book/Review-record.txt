CONSTRUCTIONS SUPPLIED IN THE BOOK
- Tape simulation by counters, finite-counter prime packing into two counters,
  the halting diagonal, and translations connecting the seven computation models.
  The distinction between plain and encoded input is explicit.
- The numeric and RAM base constructions used by the lift theorem.
- Every block of the universal programs U and U_P, with exact expansions,
  addresses, register allocation, instruction coding, and the simulation argument.
- PSD-to-Gram factorization, four explicit Clifford matrices and their measurement
  construction, both directions of the arcsine characterization, and an explicit
  finite local distribution satisfying the four prescribed correlators.
- Smaller missing definitions and arguments, including the sorted-list checker.

The elliptope certificate test is called a certificate checker, not a decider for
the entire set. Soundness does not establish completeness. The manuscript retains
its stated physical premises, open questions and limits.

VALIDATION
All 256 numbered mathematical items remain in the same order. All 51 existing
standalone displayed equations from the preceding manuscript pass are retained,
ignoring whitespace. There are no unresolved internal references. One proof
environment was added to the existing lift theorem; the other new arguments appear
in the new appendix subsections.

An independent expansion of the printed universal blocks matches every instruction
and target in the existing program data: U has 3,728 instructions and 69 paid sites;
U_P has 3,735 instructions and 70 paid sites. Exact symbolic checks confirm the
Clifford identities and triple-distribution marginals. Finite numerical checks
exercise the interval constructions, including degenerate angles. These checks
supplement the written arguments; they are not a proof of the entire manuscript.

Both PDF settings build without overfull boxes, missing glyphs, unresolved
references or off-page text. Selected new mathematical and instruction-table pages
were visually inspected. Both PDFs identify Devon Thiele as their author. The two
edition consistency tests pass. No formal proof source was changed.

The old source-citation heuristics now see no implementation citations in the book;
their empty results do not constitute mathematical validation.
