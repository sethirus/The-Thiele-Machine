START HERE

The-Thiele-Machine-reading-copy.pdf contains 32 pages for review. These
are unchanged pages extracted from the complete setting, in their original
order and on their original left or right side. It includes the opening,
the account of authorship, a continuous proof passage, the checking chapter,
and the conclusion's return to the warehouse. It is an excerpt, not a
replacement manuscript. Its bookmarks identify each selected run.

The-Thiele-Machine-book-design.pdf is the complete 668-page setting.
This is a design draft, not the released book or a claim that the artistic
brief has been fully achieved. All authorship is Devon Thiele's.

VIEWING AND PRINTING

Use facing pages with the first page displayed on its own. Both PDFs request
that view, although some readers ignore the preference. Page size is
190 x 255 mm. Print at actual size, duplex, flipping on the long edge.
A4 and US Letter can accommodate that page without shrinking it. The
composition and sequence are identical in the PDF and an ordinary printout.

The reading copy keeps the original printed folios, so they jump between
excerpts. Internal cross-references refer to the complete book. Use the full
PDF for its 273 bookmarks and 2,598 links. The excerpt deliberately contains
no copied links that might point to a page excluded from it.

Paper, cover construction, binding, and a physical proof are still needed
to judge handling, opacity, opening behavior, weight, and surface. This
draft establishes the interior composition and title page. It is not a
printer-specific production package or a finished cover wrap.

COMPOSITION

The book uses a stable Palatino reading surface, upright formal statements,
small running matter at the outer bottom edge, and part openings on the
left facing the first chapter on the right. The opening warehouse, the
account of authorship and refusal, the checker's limits, and the final
return share a lowered entry position. No explanatory art text has been
added to the manuscript.

The layout keeps mathematical qualifications in the argument's reading
surface. Sources use a natural right edge. Long printed paths can break at
directory separators, and the inline list of matrix indices can wrap. A
few local line and page adjustments prevent crowded lines and stranded
chapter endings. construction.json records every intervention.

VERIFICATION

The manuscript body can be recovered exactly by removing the recorded
formatting interventions. All 311 label values extracted from the original
auxiliary file retain their numbers. The final build has no overfull boxes,
missing glyphs, or undefined references. Text remains within the physical
page and all 17 fonts are embedded. The extracted pages render identically
to their complete-book counterparts at the checked resolution.

verification.json records the checks and their limits. Fifteen underfull
line warnings remain, mainly around technical source names and references.
Two package compatibility warnings remain; they concern an unused tocloft
hook and microtype's footnote patch. Contents, bookmarks, and the resulting
pages were inspected. These checks establish construction, not artistic
success or a physically proofed object.

REBUILD

From this directory, with Python 3 and pdfLaTeX available:

    python build.py
    python review.py

The first command reads ../monograph.tex, applies reversible composition
hooks, and runs three typesetting passes. It does not edit that manuscript.
The second needs PyMuPDF (tested with 1.26.6) and ../monograph.aux, checks
the book, and produces the reading copy and pagination survey. Generated
TeX intermediates and inspection images go in the ignored build directory.

The setting uses TeX Live's article, mathpazo, microtype, geometry, amsthm,
titlesec, fancyhdr, tocloft, needspace, ragged2e, and the original manuscript's
other packages. No downloaded images or external font files are required.
