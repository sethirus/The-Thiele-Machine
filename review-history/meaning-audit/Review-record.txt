Principal corrections
1. A finite permanent flag-raising instruction must merge some whole states.
   The newly entering state need not itself share a destination with another.
   Reading the flag alone does not establish a collision of whole states.
2. Actual entropy loss depends on the prepared inputs. A known deterministic
   start has zero logical entropy loss; this gives no positive lower bound
   from the adopted entropy-loss premise, not a promise of zero device heat.
3. Merge pricing, halving pricing and entropy pricing were being presented
   as interchangeable. Halving and worst-case entropy pricing are equivalent
   in the stated finite setting; merge pricing is their weaker restriction
   to pairs. A one-mark merge charge cannot bound an arbitrary-size squeeze.
4. Abstract ledger charges, mean heat, work and total entropy production are
   distinct quantities. Physical interpretations now retain their conditions.
   The time-symmetric control claim was checked against the cited primary
   paper. The fixed-rate relaxation bound no longer implies a heat charge
   on each flip or identifies itself with absolute irreversibility.
5. Corrections elsewhere include the distinction between a missing check and
   a missing payment; a uniform-over-keys refinement result; noninterference
   of the base projection; effective rather than arbitrary translations;
   functional fixed points; host capability versus the particular universal
   program; trace cost versus stored-program cost; and conditions for sharp
   numerical bounds.
6. These distinctions have been propagated into the opening, chapter summaries,
   conclusion, glossary, assumptions, contribution statements and claim ledger.
